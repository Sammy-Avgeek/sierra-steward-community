Community Console Terms of Use
Version 1.0

You may install and use Community Console to operate Discord communities you are authorised to manage. Follow Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and Developer Policy. Keep your Discord bot token private.

Community Console stores its settings, token, warning history, and ticket records on this computer. When the bot is connected, it sends messages and other requests to Discord using your bot token.
